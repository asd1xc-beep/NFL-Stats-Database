Call codes

Rule: the call code is the player's jersey number, two digits, with the action prefix in front. Our team is always 3 digits and the opponent is always 4 — this is a broadcast-side rule, not a home/away one, so it holds whether we're at home or on the road. Exceptions are only #0 and shared numbers; those are listed below.
